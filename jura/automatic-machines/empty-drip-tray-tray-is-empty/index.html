Jura Empty drip tray (tray is empty): false drip-tray message

Empty drip tray (tray is empty) : Tray message will not clear, or "Tray missing" with the tray fitted.

Réponse rapide. The tray is detected and its fullness measured through two metal contact strips on the back of the tray. Coffee residue, milk or scale bridging them reads as full; corrosion or a bent strip reads as missing.

Quand l’erreur survientà tout moment

Comment corriger Empty drip tray (tray is empty)

  1. Remove the tray, wash it, and wipe the two metal strips dry with a damp cloth, then dry.
  2. Wipe the mating contacts in the machine.
  3. Refit the tray firmly until it clicks.
  4. If it still reads full, the strips are corroded through; a new tray is the fix.

Questions fréquentes

Que signifie Empty drip tray (tray is empty) sur la Jura ?

Tray message will not clear, or "Tray missing" with the tray fitted. The tray is detected and its fullness measured through two metal contact strips on the back of the tray. Coffee residue, milk or scale bridging them reads as full; corrosion or a bent strip reads as missing.

Difficulté : facile. Remove the tray, wash it, and wipe the two metal strips dry with a damp cloth, then dry. Wipe the mating contacts in the machine.
